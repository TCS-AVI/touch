Exam practice

A timed run on story and report passages, like the real test. You get gross and net WPM, key depressions per hour, and error rate. English and Hindi (Inscript) are supported; Urdu and Kruti Dev are not.

Dictation window (steno practice)

Listen, jot outlines on the pad or on paper, then type what you heard. Outlines are drawn by hand, so this app does not teach Pitman or Gregg; it trains the dictation-and-transcription skill that steno tests measure.

Stage 0 · Set up your body and your hands

  • Sit upright, feet flat, elbows near 90°, wrists floating above the desk.
  • Put your screen at eye level, an arm’s length away.
  • Rest your left fingers on A S D F and your right on J K L ;. Thumbs hover over the space bar. Feel the bumps on F and J.
  • Every finger owns a column of keys; the colors below show which finger. Return to the home row after each key.
  • Never look at the keys. Look at the text. Slow and accurate now means fast later.

This build teaches QWERTY. Dvorak and Colemak are not included yet.

Privacy: what is stored

Everything is stored only in this browser, on your device: your name or nickname, your goal and target speed, your lesson progress, your settings, per-key speed and error statistics, and a practice log (date, minutes, speed, accuracy). The raw keystrokes of a run are kept in memory only while you practise and are not saved. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.

Privacy: what leaves your device

Your practice data is never sent to The Computer Services or to anyone else. The page asks Google Fonts for one font (Atkinson Hyperlegible), so Google can see that request, as it would for any website that uses its fonts. Offline, the page uses your system font. Touch is hosted on GitHub Pages (tcs-avi.github.io). GitHub can see that you visited, as any web host can, and handles that under its own privacy statement. After your first visit your browser may also keep a copy of the app on your device so it works offline; clearing your browser’s site data removes it. The dictation tab uses your browser’s built-in speech voices. In some browsers those voices run online, and the text spoken is built-in sample text, never your own typing.

Your control

Export backup (in My progress & backup) creates a text copy of your data, including your name, so keep it private. Clearing your browser’s site data erases everything, and so does the button below. On a shared computer, use a nickname or delete your data when you finish.
